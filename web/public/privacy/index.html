Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 19, 2026 • Published by: Lorapok Labs • Contact: privacy@lorapok.tech

1. Introduction

Lorapok Labs operates NoCatch (https://nocatch.lorapok.tech), the NoCatch Discord Application, the NoCatch Browser Extension, and the @lorapok/nocatch-core software suite.

Core Commitment: We adhere to a strict Zero-Trust, Zero-Retention architectural philosophy. We do not harvest, store, or sell your personal data, media, or credentials.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Media Content Storage: We do not store, mirror, index, or archive any podcasts, audio files, video streams, or markdown notes downloaded through NoCatch. All media streams pipe directly between Google's content delivery networks and your client device.
  • No Personal Google Data: We do not log, track, or save your Google account credentials, passwords, or personal files.
  • No IP Address or Tracking Logs: We do not sell, rent, or monetize your IP address or browser telemetry.
  • No Discord User Data: The NoCatch Discord bot does not store user message history, personal chat logs, or user credentials.

3. Information Handled Ephemerally

  • Notebook URLs: When you provide a Google NotebookLM URL or artifact UUID to the web interface, API, or Discord bot, it is processed in temporary volatile memory solely to resolve the underlying Google CDN streaming link. It is discarded immediately after the response is returned.
  • Google Session Cookies (Optional): If you provide an optional session cookie to harvest a private notebook owned by your account, that cookie is held strictly in volatile RAM for the duration of the RPC handshake and never persisted to disk or databases.
  • Client-Side ZIP Generation: When you click "Download All (ZIP)", the entire archive packaging process is performed client-side inside your browser engine using JSZip. No files are uploaded to our servers for bundling.

4. Discord Bot Data Handling

The NoCatch Discord Application operates under strict compliance with Discord's Developer Terms of Service and Developer Policy:

  • Intents: The bot only processes messages containing explicit slash commands (/nocatch audio, /nocatch video, /nocatch all) or explicit user interactions.
  • Data Retention: No message content, channel logs, or member IDs are stored in persistent databases.
  • Permissions: The bot requests only minimal necessary permissions: Send Messages, Embed Links, Attach Files, and Use Slash Commands.

5. Third-Party Services

NoCatch communicates with:

  • Google Cloud / Google NotebookLM CDN: To resolve and fetch requested media streams.
  • Cloudflare Pages: For secure, edge-rendered web application delivery protected by Cloudflare Zero-Trust infrastructure.
  • Discord API: For delivering slash commands and player embeds within Discord guilds and direct messages.

6. Children's Privacy

NoCatch does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 13. If you believe we have received any such data, please contact us immediately.

7. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published directly at https://nocatch.lorapok.tech/privacy and documented in the public GitHub repository.
